WN18RR (Dettmers et al., "Convolutional 2D Knowledge Graph Embeddings", AAAI 2018),
derived from WordNet (Princeton University).

These three files are the standard WN18RR split (86,835 / 3,034 / 3,134 triples,
40,943 entities, 11 relations).  Their content (sorted triples) is identical to
  - github.com/DeepGraphLearning/KnowledgeGraphEmbedding, data/wn18rr
    (the RotatE authors' repository)
  - github.com/villmow/datasets_knowledge_embedding, WN18RR/original
and is checked again by wn18rr_prepare.py through content hashes.
