WN18RR files (folders wn18rr_original, wn18rr_O, wn18rr_L)

WN18RR was introduced by T. Dettmers, P. Minervini, P. Stenetorp and
S. Riedel, "Convolutional 2D Knowledge Graph Embeddings", AAAI 2018, and is
derived from WordNet 3.0 (Princeton University). wn18rr_original holds the
standard files unchanged (see wn18rr_original/SOURCE.txt); wn18rr_O is the
same split and wn18rr_L a re-split of the same triples, both written by
code/wn18rr_prepare.py. WordNet is distributed under the following license.
